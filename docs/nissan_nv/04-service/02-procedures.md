# 4.2 Procedures {#procedures}

---

## Throttle and idle relearn {#relearn}

Run this after any work on the accelerator pedal, MAF, or throttle body, and
after disconnecting any of them. The Innova 5610 can automate it (Special
Functions).

Bring the engine to operating temperature, then shut it off. Do all three
steps in order.

1. **Accelerator pedal released position.** Foot off the gas. Key ON 2 s, OFF
   10 s, ON 2 s, OFF 10 s.
2. **Closed throttle position.** Foot off the gas. Key ON, then OFF, and wait
   10 s. You should hear the throttle body move.
3. **Idle air volume learning.** Key ON and start a timer.
    - At 3 s, pump the pedal fully 5 times within 5 s.
    - At 10 s, floor the pedal and hold it. The check engine light blinks,
      then goes solid. Release within 3 s.
    - Start the engine and let it idle for 30 s.

| Check | Value |
| :---- | :---- |
| Target idle | Under 850 RPM |
| This van, after a good relearn | 638 RPM |
| If it fails | Key off, wait 60 s, and start again from step 1 |

## Oil change {#oil-change}

| Item | Spec |
| :--- | :--- |
| Oil | Mobil 1 0W-40 European Car Formula (step up: HPL Premium Plus) |
| Filter | Fram Ultra XG9688 or Wix XP 57502 |
| Capacity | 6.9 qt with filter. Buy 7, add about 6.5, run briefly, then top off to full |
| Interval | 4,000 miles |
| Oil analysis | Used oil analysis (UOA) at every change, to watch for cylinder 7 wear |
