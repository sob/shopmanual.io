| Item | Value |

| Mileage | About 95k (September 2026), out of warranty |
| Suspension / tires | Lifted, 35" tires |

!!! warning "The previous owner fitted a pedal commander"
    A pedal commander installed by the previous owner hid a failing
    accelerator pedal assembly until March 2026 (see
    [Service History](01-service-history.md#2026-03)). Treat other
    previous-owner changes as unverified.
